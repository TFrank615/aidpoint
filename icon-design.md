# AidPoint app icon

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. The artwork is a white location pin with a white aid plus on a cobalt and navy background. The master is preserved in `aidpoint-icon-master.png`; opaque PNG exports are `apple-touch-icon.png` (180 × 180), `icon-192.png`, and `icon-512.png`.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: AidPoint iPhone Home Screen icon, final production artwork as a square opaque PNG, ideally 1024 by 1024 pixels. Primary request: a polished, instantly recognizable app icon for AidPoint, a tool that helps emergency dispatchers and fire/EMS personnel locate the closest mutual aid to an incident. Subject: one bold white location pin silhouette; inside the pin's circular cobalt-blue center, a crisp white plus symbol signifies aid. Clean custom geometry with smooth curves, strong balanced proportions, visually centered with generous safe margins (all meaningful artwork inside the middle 70% of the canvas). Background: full bleed deep navy blue, with a restrained cobalt glow behind the symbol. Palette: deep navy, cobalt blue, white; this matches the app's blue search controls. Style: premium contemporary app branding, confident minimal flat vector-like artwork, highly legible at tiny sizes. Constraints: icon artwork only, fill the entire square; no text, no letters, no apple logo, no watermark, no mockup, no phone, no frames, no baked-in rounded corners, no transparent areas, no tiny details, no exaggerated 3D rendering, no busy emergency-service emblem. White plus, not a red cross.
